Questions before you call?

Can you help with a backed-up main drain?

Tell us which fixtures are backing up and whether a cleanout is accessible. We'll discuss the appropriate next step.

When is hydro jetting appropriate?

It depends on the blockage, pipe condition and available access. We can discuss whether it fits your situation.

Do you also service heating and AC?

Yes, KAA Services accepts inquiries about residential heating, cooling and HVAC maintenance.

Is same-day service guaranteed?

No. Availability varies by schedule and location. Call or text to check the earliest opening.

How much will my service cost?

It depends on the problem and scope. Ask about diagnostic or visit charges when scheduling; any proposed work should be quoted before it begins.
